Digital Out-of-Home · 29 Sep 2026

Screens are winning the street.

Digital billboards are the fastest-growing part of outdoor advertising. Here is why, and what it means for brands in Kingston.

14.5%projected 2026 growth in US digital billboard spend

US outdoor advertising is on track for about US$4 billion in agency-tracked spend this year, up 4.1%, according to media intelligence firm Guideline. The growth is not evenly spread. Digital screens are projected to grow 14.5%. Traditional printed billboards: 1.5%.

One caveat is worth keeping. Outdoor's overall share of US media spend has slipped since 2022, from 3.1% to 2.7%, and Guideline found that most digital growth is new money entering the category rather than budgets moving over from static boards. Screens are not so much replacing print as attracting advertisers who were not buying outdoor at all. Digital's growth is also slowing from recent years, and Guideline points to a shortage of available screens as the main brake.

Why screens pull in new advertisers

  • No print, no install. Creative goes live when the file is ready, not when the vinyl is.
  • The message can change by the day. A weekend promotion, a Monday launch and a same-day event reminder can all run on the same board.
  • The location is shared. Several brands rotate on one screen, so a strong spot costs a fraction of owning a static face.
  • Creative can be tested and swapped. If a message is not landing, replace it instead of living with it for a month.

What it means in Kingston

The same logic works at a local scale. The advertisers who gain most from a screen are the ones a static billboard never suited: restaurants with daily specials, events with a countdown, retailers with a weekend sale, and brands that want presence on a busy road without locking into one creative for months.

Two things decide whether it works: a location with real daily traffic, and creative built for a glance. That means one message, big type and a clear next step.

The takeaway

Screens win because they are flexible, not because they are digital. Buy the flexibility: short flights, timely messages and fresh creative.
