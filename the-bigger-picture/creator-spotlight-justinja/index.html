Creator Spotlight: JustInJA and the Jamaica beyond the resort gate.

Justin Whitelocke built a following of more than 300,000 across Instagram and TikTok by showing people the parts of the island most visitors never reach.

Justin Whitelocke's Jamaica has Maroon villages, family farms, the Blue Mountains and community-run eco-adventures in it. As @just_in_ja, the Kingston-based creator has built an audience of more than 237,000 on Instagram and 81,000 on TikTok by making those places feel close enough to visit.

In 2025 the Jamaica Tourist Board chose Justin to host "There's Always More to Jamaica", a digital campaign aimed at travellers in the US, Canada, the UK, Germany and France. The campaign showcased experiences like cooking callaloo with farming families near Montego Bay, basket weaving with local craftspeople and early-morning trips out with fishermen.

His advice to visitors is simple, and it sums up the content: talk to a local first.

Why brands work with him

  • He makes a place the hero. That suits hospitality, attractions, food, transport and any brand with a location worth visiting.
  • His audience engages. His Instagram engagement rate is 6.3%, and 65% of his audience is women.
  • He has delivered at national scale. He hosted a Tourist Board campaign aimed at five overseas markets.
The takeaway

If your brand has a place to show off, Justin turns it into a story people want to go and see.
