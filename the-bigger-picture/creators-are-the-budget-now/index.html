Creator Economy · 29 Sep 2026

Creators are not the experiment anymore. They are the budget.

US creator marketing spend is forecast to pass US$21 billion in 2026. The bigger change is where that content ends up.

$21.1Bforecast US creator marketing spend in 2026

EMARKETER forecasts that US brands will spend US$21.1 billion on creator marketing in 2026, more than double the 2022 level. Its analysts describe creators as having moved from a novelty to a core part of the plan, and IAB research from late 2025 found that 48% of marketers now consider creators essential.

The more useful signal is what brands are doing with the content. Budgets are tilting towards smaller creators, and creator content is being reused well beyond social feeds: in retail media, on connected TV and in-store.

What that means for campaigns here

  • Engagement over reach. A creator with a smaller, loyal audience often moves more people than a bigger account with a passive one.
  • Plan for reuse from day one. If you want to run a creator's video as a paid ad, on a screen or in-store, agree usage rights in the brief. Organic posting does not include them by default.
  • Brief for the creator's voice. The content that performs sounds like the creator, not like the brand's ad copy.
  • Ask for the numbers. Reach, views, engagement and, where possible, clicks or redemptions, reported on a set schedule.
The takeaway

Treat creator content as an asset, not a post. Brief for reuse, pay for the rights and measure the results.
